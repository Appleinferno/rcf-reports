So we made a deal to buy that 3 1/4" wide prefinished Hickory. And we sell it for a bargain at $2.99 sq ft!

3. Purchase Factory Returned Wood Flooring

Another opportunity to save serious money on hardwood floors is by purchasing our factory returns from Somerset and Bruce. We currently liquidate all of the returns nationwide from Somerset, as well as  factory returns from AHF on Bruce, Hartco and Robbins flooring. These returns typically are because of someone didn't like the color. Or they changed their mind. Occasionally there is an actual or perceived defect that is so slight no one would know, but we put that defect as stated in our notes on the website. This inventory allows us to sell hardwood floors that appear to be perfect for a fraction of the price the floor originally sold for.

4. FLower Grades Of Wood Floors For Any Budget

One of the quirks about wood flooring is that a manufacturer will have at least 2 0r 3 grades of wood flooring after the product is finished. One of our manufacturers has 5! We work with these companies to move these sub-standard grades so they can focus on their standard graded inventory, as that will represent about 90% of their inventory. Grades like Cabin, Builder, and Utility offer huge savings to the customers that are willing to accept natural or manufacturing defects and shorter board lengths, depending on the grade.
